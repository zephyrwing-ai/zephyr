A quiet interface still has things to say. It shows what can be clicked, where the reader is, and what an action will do. Each cue appears where it can be useful.

## Align things that belong together

Navigation labels share a starting point. Headings and paragraphs follow the same axis. Alignment gives the eye a steady reference and leaves more attention for the content.

Dates and labels stay close to the things they describe, forming small, complete groups.

## Give each state a place

The selected section, open article, and current heading belong to three different levels. A shared visual language can connect them while each remains in its own navigation area.

Feedback becomes part of the page's structure.

## Make the details comfortable

A visible focus ring, reachable controls, and readable contrast shape everyday use. They deserve the same attention as color and type.

The interface feels quiet when each element has a clear job and performs it well.
