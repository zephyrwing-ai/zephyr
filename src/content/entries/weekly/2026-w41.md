Space expresses relationships. Nearby elements feel connected; larger gaps mark a change of purpose. Readers sense this structure before reading a word.

## Begin with a paragraph

The gap between a heading and its opening paragraph helps them read as a unit. Space between paragraphs offers a pause as one thought gives way to another.

A consistent spacing scale gives articles of different lengths a familiar rhythm.

## Give the article priority

Navigation, dates, and outlines support reading. Quieter colors and compact spacing let them remain useful while the article holds the visual center.

A clear hierarchy makes the next step easier to find.

## Try another screen

On a narrow screen, the relationships between text, images, and navigation change. Moving the article list into a menu and the outline above the text preserves the reading path.

Continuity comes from relationships that still make sense as the available space changes.
