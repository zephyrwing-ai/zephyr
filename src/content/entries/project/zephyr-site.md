A personal site gives ideas somewhere to live. Weekly notes capture a moment in time. Everyday observations follow a thread of curiosity. Project pages keep the work and its decisions together.

![The zephyr site with an article list, reading column, and page outline.](/images/zephyr-site-preview.svg)

## A home for the work

Each piece has a clear place and an address that can be shared. Over time, individual entries form a record of questions, observations, and things made.

Keeping the site current begins with writing: add a note, choose an image, and publish when it feels ready.

## Let content lead

Sections sit at the top, articles on the left, and the outline on the right. The reading column gives the content room to unfold.

On a phone, the article takes priority. Navigation remains close at hand through a compact menu and outline.

## Keep the rhythm

A restrained palette and consistent type establish a familiar rhythm across short notes and longer articles. Images create a pause; headings make the structure easy to scan.

The layout should feel comfortable enough for a quick visit and a longer read.

## Leave room to grow

The structure leaves room for new subjects and projects. Each addition can find its place while the reading experience stays familiar.
